Geyser → Kafka → ClickHouse: production notes

Throughput tuning I’ve used in production pipelines:

  1. Shard Kafka by slot to keep ordering guarantees.
  2. Use bounded channels between gRPC stream handlers and producers.
  3. Materialized views in ClickHouse for hot queries; aggregate tables for long-tail analytics.
  4. Checkpointing by slot/root to recover quickly after reorgs.
