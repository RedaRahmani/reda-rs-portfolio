Reorg-safe ingestion: deterministic IDs + gap repair

This note sketches how I keep Solana ingest stable through forks: deterministic IDs derived from slot/tx indexes, gap repair workers, and idempotent writes.

  • Deterministic IDs prevent duplicates after rollback.
  • Gap repair worker replays missing slots using RPC with bounded fetch size.
  • Writes are idempotent and keyed by slot+index, so reruns are safe.
